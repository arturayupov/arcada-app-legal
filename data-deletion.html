Data Deletion Instructions

Arcada Studio apps store only the data needed to publish to accounts we own (OAuth tokens, basic account identifiers, and records of the posts we created). To have this data deleted:

  1. Revoke access on the platform — TikTok: Profile → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions → Arcada Studio Poster → Remove access. Facebook/Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → remove the app. Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation immediately invalidates our tokens.
  2. Request deletion from us — email arturayupov@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and the platform username. We delete the stored tokens, identifiers and post records within 7 days and confirm by email.

Content that was already published to a platform is governed by that platform and can be deleted from the platform's own interface.
