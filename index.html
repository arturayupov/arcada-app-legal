Arcada Studio Poster

Arcada Studio Poster is a private, self-hosted publishing tool operated by Arcada Studio (Artur Ayupov / Arcada US LLC). It schedules and publishes our own short-form videos — produced by our in-house video pipeline — to the social media accounts that we own and operate (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, X, LinkedIn).

How it works

  1. A member of our team signs in to the tool and connects one of our own social accounts through the platform's official OAuth login (for example, TikTok Login Kit).
  2. Our video pipeline renders a finished video (MP4) and its caption.
  3. The tool uploads the video to the connected account through the platform's official API (for example, TikTok Content Posting API) — either as a direct post or as a draft that we finish in the platform's app.
  4. We can review the post status in the tool's dashboard.

Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. Registration is disabled; only our team members have accounts, and only accounts owned by Arcada Studio are connected.
