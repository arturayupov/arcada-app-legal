Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026. Applies to Arcada Studio Poster and other applications operated by Arcada Studio (the “Apps”).

1. Who we are

The Apps are operated by Artur Ayupov, doing business as Arcada Studio, through Arcada US LLC (“we”, “us”). Contact: arturayupov@gmail.com.

2. What the Apps do

The Apps are internal tools that publish content we create to social media accounts that we own and operate, using the official APIs of TikTok, Meta (Instagram, Facebook, Threads), Google (YouTube), X and LinkedIn (“Platforms”). They are not offered to the general public.

3. Data we process

  • Authentication data issued by a Platform when a team member connects an account: OAuth access and refresh tokens, the account's Platform user ID (e.g. TikTok open_id / union_id), display name, username and avatar URL.
  • Content data: the videos, captions, hashtags and thumbnails that we create and upload, and the identifiers and status of the resulting posts returned by the Platform.
  • Technical logs: server logs (timestamps, request status, IP address of our own team members) kept for troubleshooting.

We do not collect data about viewers, followers or any third-party users of the Platforms beyond aggregated statistics the Platform may return for our own accounts.

4. Why we process it (legal basis)

To publish and manage our own content on our own accounts (performance of our internal operations / legitimate interest). We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored on servers we control, hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany/Finland). Tokens are stored encrypted at rest in the App's database; access is limited to our team over authenticated connections (HTTPS/SSH).

6. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. Data is transmitted only to the Platform that issued it, strictly to perform the publishing actions described above, and to our hosting provider as a processor.

7. Retention

OAuth tokens are kept while an account stays connected and are deleted when the account is disconnected or the token is revoked. Post records and logs are retained for up to 12 months and then deleted.

8. Your rights and data deletion

Because the Apps are used only by our own team on our own accounts, data subjects are our team members. Anyone whose data is held by the Apps may request access, correction or deletion at any time — see our Data Deletion Instructions. You can also revoke the App's access from the Platform's own settings (for example, TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Facebook/Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites), which immediately invalidates our tokens.

9. Platform terms

Use of TikTok data is subject to the TikTok Privacy Policy; use of Meta data is subject to the Meta Privacy Policy; use of YouTube data is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

10. Children

The Apps are not directed to children and we do not knowingly process data of anyone under 18.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly. The current version is always published at this URL.
